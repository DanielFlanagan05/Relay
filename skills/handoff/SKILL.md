---
name: Context handoff
description: Preserve the decisions and next steps
---

Summarize the objective, decisions, relevant files, completed work, constraints, validation, unresolved problems, and concrete next steps. Preserve source references and distinguish facts from assumptions. Keep the summary sufficient for a fresh session.

Name the evidence for any restart, compaction, or interrupted edit. Do not invent missing history to explain an empty status file. Include the latest known test result and the exact next check, so continuation does not repeat broad exploration.
